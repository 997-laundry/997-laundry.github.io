Privacy Policy — 997 Laundry

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number, and address or location when you request pickup or delivery.
  • Order details (items, services, amounts, status) and prepaid packages.
  • WhatsApp messages you send to the shop's number and our replies.

How we use it

  • To fulfil your orders: pickup, cleaning, delivery, invoices and receipts.
  • To answer you on WhatsApp, including an automated assistant that replies from the shop's own data (prices, packages, your order status) and hands you to a person on request.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it for marketing. Messages are processed only by services needed to run the service: WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta), our system database (Supabase), and the Claude AI model (Anthropic) to write the assistant's replies.

Security and retention

Access is limited to shop staff. We keep data while you are a customer and as required by accounting rules in Bahrain.

Your rights and data deletion

To get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it, send “delete my data” on WhatsApp to +973 3959 9902. We respond within 30 days (keeping only financial records required by law).
